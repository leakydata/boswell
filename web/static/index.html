The charger defaults to 50 mA, so a 500 mAh cell takes about ten hours. 100 mA halves that. Only raise it on a cell rated for the current.
Dimming is real PWM, so current falls with brightness. Below a few percent the PWM hardware costs more than the light does — blinking briefly is cheaper than a very dim steady glow, and off costs nothing at all.
Off: finish the buffered audio first, so the conversation stays in order. On: hear live audio immediately and recover the buffer alongside it — recovered audio is saved as its own clip.
