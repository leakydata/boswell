The charger defaults to 50 mA, so a 500 mAh cell takes about ten hours. 100 mA halves that. Only raise it on a cell rated for the current.
The light blinks briefly by default — under 1% of the power a steady one uses, and still enough to show what the device is doing. Turn this on to keep it lit, and use the slider to dim it: dimming is real PWM, so current falls with brightness, though below a few percent the PWM hardware costs more than the light does. Off costs nothing.

Enrolled voices

Every label is kept as its own voiceprint — never averaged. A voice sounds different in a quiet room, outdoors, or over a bad connection, and matching takes the closest single voiceprint, so each one you add covers a condition the others do not. Remove one if it was the wrong person.

Unidentified voices

Voices that recur but nobody has named, grouped so one name settles all of them at once. Listen to the voice on its own — their turns spliced together, without anyone else — then name it. Every naming also teaches the matcher a condition it could not recognise before.

Custom words

Names, jargon, drug names — anything the transcriber gets wrong. These are boosted while it listens and corrected afterwards, so met form in comes back as the real word. Enrolled people are included automatically. One per line.

From your conversations

Clear out recordings that hold nothing

Every group below is a set of recordings with no transcript, grouped by exactly what the sound tagger heard in them. Tick the ones that mean nothing to you. A recording is only ever removed when its whole description is things you ticked — a clip tagged Typing and Dog is not a typing clip, and the dog is why it exists.

Anything with a voice in it never appears here at all. Every deletion writes a record to data/deleted/ first: what was removed, when it was recorded, and what was heard in it.
